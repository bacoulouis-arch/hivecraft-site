Support

For help with Hivecraft, write to hivecraft@icloud.com. Say which device you use and which version of iOS or iPadOS it runs.

The Field Guide

The Field Guide explains every rule and every threat in the game. Open it with the Field Guide button at the top right of the Nest, Map, Policy and Year screens.

Recovering a colony from Saved colonies

Saved colonies is the archive button at the top right of the same four screens. It is also in Settings, the gear at the top left, under This colony. It lists two kinds of file kept on your device:

  • a “Preserved original”: if the game cannot open the colony it saved, it keeps that file unchanged and opens a new colony in its place;
  • a “Before a restoration” backup: restoring a colony first saves the colony you had open.

To bring one back:

  1. Open Saved colonies and choose Preview save on the file. The preview shows the moment it keeps and each colony’s bees, honey and pollen, and changes nothing.
  2. Choose Restore this save, then Back up and restore. The colony you have open is backed up first. Neither file is deleted.
  3. The restored colony resumes at the moment it was saved. The time that has passed since then is not added.

If a file cannot be read, Preview save says so and nothing is replaced. Export file lets you send it to the developer at hivecraft@icloud.com.

Deleting, reinstalling and backups

Deleting the game, then installing it again, does not bring a colony back: the game starts a new one. Saved colonies lives in the game’s storage too, so its files go with the game.

Offload App is different. It removes the app but keeps its documents and data, and iOS restores them when you reinstall.

Restoring your device from a backup that was made while the colony was on it does bring the colony back.

Supported devices

Hivecraft runs on iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, or later. It needs no account and no network connection, and it is in English and French.
