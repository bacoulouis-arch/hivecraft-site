Privacy policy

Updated 10 November 2026

Hivecraft collects nothing and shares nothing with anyone.

The game has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it sends nothing over the network.

Where your colony is kept

Your colony is stored on your device, in the game’s own storage. The developer does not receive it.

If you back up your device, to iCloud or to a computer, the backup includes the game’s data, so your colony is in it. That backup is yours, not the developer’s: your device makes it and it is kept in your iCloud or on your computer. The developer cannot see it.

Apart from a backup, a copy of your colony leaves your device only if you export it. In the game, Saved colonies has an Export file button that opens the system share sheet, and you choose where the copy goes.

Deleting the game

Deleting the game deletes the colony from your device. A backup made before that still holds its own copy, and restoring your device from that backup brings the colony back.

What Apple may share with the developer

Apple may give the developer crash and usage figures from people who chose to share them with app developers in their device’s settings. The game does not collect or send these figures; Apple does, under its own terms.
